Why Your Gift Matters

The Friends of the Anoka County Library is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that raises funds for and increases awareness of the Anoka County Library system. Every dollar donated goes directly toward programs and resources that connect our community to ideas, information, and inspiration.

Your generosity has made a real difference — over each of the past three years, Friends supporters have helped us donate more than $20,000 to the libraries. Those funds have supported the Summer Reading Program, the ACL Local Author Fair, the Book Club-in-a-Bag collection, the Reading Bridge program for struggling readers, and staff appreciation efforts across the system.

Whether you give $5 or $500, your contribution helps ensure that the Anoka County Library remains a vibrant, welcoming resource for everyone in our community. Thank you for giving.
