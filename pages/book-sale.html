Book Sale

Thousands of gently used books at great prices — every dollar supports the Anoka County Library

Friends of the Anoka County Library holds book sales three times a year — fall, winter, and spring. Each sale offers thousands of gently used books, DVDs, and CDs sorted by genre, with prices starting at fifty cents. Every dollar raised goes directly toward funding library programs, materials, and services.

Next Sale

Details for our next book sale are coming soon. Check back here or watch our events calendar for the date, location, and hours once they're announced.

What to Expect

  • Thousands of gently used books, DVDs, and CDs sorted by genre
  • Prices starting at $0.50
  • Cash and check accepted
  • 100% of proceeds benefit the Anoka County Library

Donate Books

We gladly accept donations of gently used books, DVDs, and CDs year-round. Email FriendsOfAnokaCoLib@gmail.com to arrange a drop-off.

Volunteer

Our book sales run on volunteer power — from setup and sorting to staffing the sale floor. Visit our volunteer page to see current opportunities and sign up.
