![Promotional image for the Summer Kick Off Party showing F1rst Wrestling performers in an outdoor ring with a referee and a seated audience.](/images/blog/summer-kickoff-wrestling.jpg)

ACL's summer kick off party will be held in the Northtown Library parking lot, June 6, noon–3 pm. There will be a family-friendly show from F1rst Wrestling, an Ice Cream Truck (sponsored by the Friends), button making, and trivia. Library staff will talk to people about summer reading and help them sign up for library cards.
