Gala Tickets Available June 1

May 23, 2026 · Friends of the Library Board

Tickets for the Second Annual "An Evening With Friends & Authors," go on sale to the public on June 1, 2026. Our keynote speaker is William Kent Krueger, author of the Cork O'Connor series. Our other featured speaker is Traci Lambrecht (PJ Tracy), author of the Monkeewrench series. Lorna Landvik, author, actor, and comedian, is returning as our Master of Ceremonies.

Before the authors' presentations, guests can enjoy a social hour with hors d'oeuvres and a cash bar, and participate in our silent auction and raffles. An elegant dinner and dessert will be served. Standard tickets are $80 and Tables of 8 are $600. We are also offering limited-availability VIP tickets to sit at an author's table for $100.

For more information, to buy tickets, and to donate to the fundraiser, visit our gala page.
