We are just over three months away from the second annual Friends of the Anoka County Library gala, and tickets are going fast! Over 100 of our 300 tickets are already sold. [Get your tickets today on our gala page](/gala.html). Don't miss "An Evening With Friends & Authors" on October 15, 2026, at the Courtyards of Andover.

Here's what's in store:

- The hilarious Lorna Landvik is back as emcee!
- Presentations by bestselling authors William Kent Krueger & Traci Lambrecht (P.J. Tracy).
- A social hour followed by dinner and dessert.
- Raffles (TV, mini-getaway).
- A silent auction filled with great local prizes.

We need your help to make the gala a success! We are still looking for sponsors, financial contributions, and auction items:

- **Sponsor or Donate Online:** Head to [our gala page](/gala.html) to contribute securely via credit card.
- **Calling Local Crafters:** Want to donate a handmade creation (quilts, woodturning, metalwork, laser-cutting, etc.)? Shoot us an email at [FriendsOfAnokaCoLib@gmail.com](mailto:FriendsOfAnokaCoLib@gmail.com) and we'll arrange a pickup!

Space is limited to 300 guests, so grab your tickets today and support our library!
