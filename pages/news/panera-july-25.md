Panera gives back 30% of sales on July 25, 2026, from 4:00 to 8:00 PM at 1536 109th Ave. NE, Blaine, MN. Use code FUNDRU at panerabread.com, in the Panera app, or at the in-café kiosk.](/images/blog/panera-july-25.png)

If you can "sandwich" a Panera visit into your busy schedule, please drop by on Saturday, July 25, at the Blaine Panera Bread (1536 109th Ave. NE). Panera is donating 30% of sales back to the Friends of the Anoka County Library!

Order online all day or drop by the store from 4–8 PM. Just be sure to show our flyer (printed or digital) at checkout so we hit our $150 minimum. Thank you for your "souper" support!
