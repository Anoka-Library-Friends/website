Friends 2026 Check Presented to ACL

May 23, 2026 · Friends of the Library Board

The Friends presented a $25,000 check to the Anoka County Library at the Library Advisory Board meeting on April 27. Present to accept the check were ACL Director, Colleen Haubner, library staff, and the Library Advisory Board. We were able to give this money thanks to our community's wonderful support. These funds support several programs, services, and collections in the libraries, such as Reading Bridge, the Summer Reading program, and Book Club in a Bag. Thank you!
