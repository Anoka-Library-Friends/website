We’ve Made Membership Easier!

Anyone donating $20.00 or more annually will automatically be added to our membership roll. This change means there are fewer forms for you to fill out if you pay by credit card (through GiveMN) or digital wallet (PayPal). It also makes it easier for us because there are no additional forms to fill out. Should you prefer to send a check, please print this form and send it to the address on the form with your check.

You will receive an email to renew your membership approximately one year after your 2026 donation. If you already donate monthly, a thank-you will be sent annually to commemorate the start of your ongoing support.

Libraries are more than just quiet places to read; they are essential community hubs that offer everything from 3D printers and career coaching to safe spaces for those in need. They are an economic treasure, returning $5.67 for every $1.00 invested, according to 2022 data from the Institute of Museums and Library Services for Anoka County.

The Friends of the Anoka County Library are dedicated to supporting and uplifting the Anoka County Library (ACL). All funds raised are donated to ACL to bolster their mission to connect the community to ideas and information. We help by co-sponsoring events with ACL, such as the local author fair, sponsor programs (e.g., the Summer Reading Program, Book Club in a Bag, etc.), and assist with library awareness-raising at community events. By uniting in our belief that the ACL is one of the county’s most valuable resources, we highlight its importance and extend its reach in our neighborhoods.

How You Can Help Power Our Mission

  • Donate: Every dollar counts. Contribute now to help us reach our goal.
  • Share: Share our campaign with your friends, family, and on social media. Your advocacy can amplify our impact.
  • Volunteer: Your time and skills can make an impact too.

Thank you for supporting our mission — your support is invaluable.
