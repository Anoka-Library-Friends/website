It's always great to see the community rally around the libraries. We raised $3,412.74 at our Spring Book Sale for our wonderful Anoka County Library! Special thanks to our many volunteers, including a dozen or more high school students who did much of the heavy lifting, and to our outreach librarians, Erin and Jessica.

We look forward to seeing you at our November 14 book sale at the Rum River Library.
