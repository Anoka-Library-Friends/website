Our Authors

Our keynote speaker, William Kent Krueger, is an acclaimed American novelist best known for his New York Times best-selling Cork O’Connor mystery series set in northern Minnesota. He has also written 5 stand-alone novels, including his award-winning Ordinary Grace and This Tender Land, which spent six months on the bestseller list. His newest book, God’s Country, will be published in August.

Traci Lambrecht is a bestselling American mystery and thriller novelist who writes under the pseudonym P. J. Tracy. For over a decade, she wrote the award-winning Monkeewrench series with her mother, PJ. After her mother’s death, she continued to work solo on this series and also expanded into the Margaret Nolan detective series. Her most recent book is The Deepest Cut.

We welcome Lorna Landvik back as our talented master of ceremonies. She is a bestselling American novelist, actress, and comedian. Ms. Landvik became a national bestseller with her character-driven books like Angry Housewives Eating Bon Bons and Oh My Stars. The most recent of her thirteen novels is Last Circle of Love.

Event Details

Our second annual An Evening With Friends & Authors will kick off with a social hour. During this time, guests will enjoy hors d’oeuvres and a cash bar, while bidding on auction items, buying raffle tickets, and visiting with our authors. Attendees will also have an opportunity to purchase books and have them personally autographed by the author. Following a welcoming social hour, join us for an elegant dinner, dessert, and our evening’s highlight: the author presentations.

Date: Thursday, October 15, 2026
Venue: Courtyards of Andover, 13545 Martin St NW, Andover, MN 55304

Ticketing opens June 1.

Event Sponsors

The Friends invite you, or your business, to become an official event sponsor. Partnering with us is a powerful way to demonstrate your support and gain visibility for you and your company. 100% of sponsorship proceeds go directly toward our fundraising goal.   • Your logo in the event program
  • Your logo on event signage and slideshow
